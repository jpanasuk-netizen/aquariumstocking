How to size it

This profile gives liters, not a gallon estimate. Seriously Fish says 24 × 12 × 12 inches (56 liters) is just about big enough for a pair. Convert with the factor stated for these profiles: 1 US gallon = 3.785 liters.

56 ÷ 3.785 = 14.80 US gallons. Rounded to the nearest gallon, 14.80 is 15.

The shopping link uses that rounded 15 gallon kit. On the unrounded 14.80, Aqueon is unambiguous: 50 watts covers 5-20 gallons, and 14.80 is inside it. The 100 watt band starts at 15 gallons. 14.80 is below 15, so the 100 watt band does not contain the converted volume. A tank sold as 15 gallons sits on the first gallon of the 100 watt band and still inside 5-20, which is why the rounding step is worth showing before anyone treats 15 as the published volume. No filter liters and no heater watts appear on this profile, so this page does not invent them and does not apply the four-to-five turnover rule as if a rating had been printed.
