What Size Heater Does a 75 Gallon Tank Need?

75 gallons is on two Aqueon lines: 200 watts (50-75) and 300 watts (65-90).

Full breakdown: aquarium heater wattage guide.

Quick answer: A 75 gallon tank is the top of the Aqueon 200 watt band (50-75 gallons) and inside the 300 watt band (65-90 gallons). The 150 watt band ends at 55 gallons, so it does not include 75. Discus, at 67.37 US gallons, fits in 75 gallons, and an oscar, at about 143 US gallons, does not.

How to size it

75 gallons closes the 200 watt band and already sits inside the 300 watt band. Aqueon lists 200 watts for 50-75 gallons and 300 watts for 65-90 gallons.

50 ≤ 75 ≤ 75, so 200 watts includes it. 65 ≤ 75 ≤ 90, so 300 watts includes it.

The shared stretch is 65 through 75 gallons. A 75 is the top gallon of that overlap. The 150 watt band ended at 55, and 75 is above 55, so the overlap to explain is 200 watts with 300 watts, not 150 with 200. That earlier overlap (50 through 55) is the 55 gallon page. The chart does not say to add 200 and 300. The links are a 200 watt heater and the next band up, 300 watts.

Discus is the species this tank adds. The published volume is 255 liters. 255 ÷ 3.785 = 67.37 US gallons, and 67.37 is not above 75, so the minimum fits. No temperature is listed on that profile. Angelfish at about 53 US gallons and 75-86°F also fits, as it already did at 55 gallons. Oscar does not: about 143 US gallons is above 75, at a listed 68-82°F. Dwarf gourami remains 56 ÷ 3.785 = 14.80 US gallons, listed at 72-81°F.

FAQ

What size heater does a 75 gallon tank need?

Aqueon lists 75 gallons under two bands: 200 watts for 50-75 gallons, and 300 watts for 65-90 gallons. The 150 watt band ends at 55 gallons and does not include 75.

Why do 200 watts and 300 watts both include 75 gallons?

The 200 watt band ends at 75 gallons and the 300 watt band starts at 65 gallons. They share 65 through 75 gallons. Aqueon does not say to add 200 and 300 together.

Do discus and oscar minimums fit a 75 gallon tank?

Discus yes: 255 liters converts to 67.37 US gallons, which is under 75. No temperature is listed for that profile. Oscar no: Seriously Fish lists about 143 US gallons at 68-82°F, which is above 75.
