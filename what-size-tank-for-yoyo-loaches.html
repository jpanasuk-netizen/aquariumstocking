What Size Tank Do Yoyo Loaches Need?

Check length and width against the base on the species profile, then plan for a group.

Full breakdown: freshwater fish size and tank data.

Quick answer: Seriously Fish lists the yo-yo loach (Botia almorhae) at up to 6 in. (160 mm) and gives a minimum tank base of 48 x 18 in. (120 x 45 cm), about 64 US gallons (243 litres), at 66 to 82 °F. It advises groups of at least 5 or 6, preferably 10 or more.

A note on the name: Seriously Fish says true Botia almorhae has possibly never been seen in the aquarium trade, and that the fish most commonly sold in this group seem to be B. lohachata, B. sp. 'Kosi' and B. sp. 'Teesta', plus hybrids. This page uses the yo-yo loach group name and the figures printed on the Botia almorhae profile.

Check a tank against 48 × 18 in.

Group size

Seriously Fish says Botia species are gregarious, form complex social hierarchies and should be kept in groups of at least 5 or 6, preferably 10 or more; kept singly, they can become withdrawn. The profile also says the tank needs a tight-fitting lid because these loaches jump, and that the species needs a well-established tank.

What this means

Aqueon's 75 gallon, at 48½ × 18½ in., clears the base; so do the 60 breeder and the 90. The 55 gallon is long enough but only 12¾ in. front to back, so it misses the width. The 40 breeder is wide enough but too short. For stocking a 75, see how many fish in a 75 gallon tank; for heating it to the 66–82 °F range, see the aquarium heater wattage guide.
