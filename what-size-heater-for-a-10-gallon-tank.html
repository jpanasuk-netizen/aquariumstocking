How to size it

Aqueon prints five heater bands and no separate row for a 10 gallon tank. The size is placed by testing 10 against each band's edges.

5 ≤ 10 ≤ 20, so 50 watts includes a 10 gallon tank. 10 < 15, so 100 watts does not.

The overlap between those two bands is the stretch from 15 gallons through 20 gallons. A 10 sits under that stretch, which is why a heater guard is the second product here instead of a 100 watt unit. The 150 watt band opens at 30, the 200 watt band at 50, and the 300 watt band at 65. Each of those floors is above 10.

FAQ

What size heater does a 10 gallon tank need?

Aqueon lists 50 watts for aquariums of 5-20 gallons. A 10 gallon tank is inside that band. The 100 watt band is 15-40 gallons, and 10 is below 15.

Do the 50 watt and 100 watt bands both cover a 10 gallon tank?

No. Those bands overlap from 15 to 20 gallons. A 10 gallon tank is below 15, so only the 50 watt band includes it.

Which species minimums in this set fit a 10 gallon tank?

None. The smallest Seriously Fish volumes here are about 11 US gallons for Betta splendens at 72-86°F and about 11 US gallons for Poecilia reticulata at 63-82°F.
