What Size Heater Does a 55 Gallon Tank Need?

55 gallons sits on two Aqueon lines: 150 watts (30-55) and 200 watts (50-75).

Full breakdown: aquarium heater wattage guide.

Quick answer: A 55 gallon tank is the top of the Aqueon 150 watt band (30-55 gallons) and the bottom edge of the 200 watt band (50-75 gallons); the 100 watt band ends at 40 and the 300 watt band starts at 65, so neither includes 55. An angelfish minimum of about 53 US gallons fits in 55 gallons. A discus minimum of 67.37 US gallons does not.

How to size it

55 gallons is the other shared edge on the chart. Aqueon ends the 150 watt band at 55 and starts the 200 watt band at 50, so 55 is inside both.

30 ≤ 55 ≤ 55, so 150 watts includes it. 50 ≤ 55 ≤ 75, so 200 watts includes it.

The shared region is 50 through 55 gallons. A 55 is the last gallon of that overlap, the same way a 40 was the last gallon of the 100/150 overlap. The 100 watt band stopped at 40, and 55 is above 40. The 300 watt band starts at 65, and 55 is below 65. The chart does not say to add 150 and 200. The links below are a 150 watt heater and the next band up, 200 watts.

This tank is the first size in the heater set that clears the angelfish minimum. About 53 US gallons is under 55, and the angelfish temperature is 75-86°F. It does not clear discus. The discus volume is 255 liters. 255 ÷ 3.785 = 67.37 US gallons, and 67.37 is above 55. Oscar remains about 143 US gallons, also above 55. Dwarf gourami stays 56 ÷ 3.785 = 14.80 US gallons, at 72-81°F.

FAQ

What size heater does a 55 gallon tank need?

Aqueon lists 55 gallons under two bands: 150 watts for 30-55 gallons, and 200 watts for 50-75 gallons. The 100 watt band ends at 40 gallons, and the 300 watt band starts at 65 gallons.

Why do 150 watts and 200 watts both include 55 gallons?

The 150 watt band ends at 55 gallons and the 200 watt band starts at 50 gallons. They share 50 through 55 gallons. Aqueon does not say to add 150 and 200 together.

Does a 55 gallon tank meet the angelfish and discus minimums?

Angelfish yes: Seriously Fish lists about 53 US gallons, which is under 55, at 75-86°F. Discus no: 255 liters converts to 67.37 US gallons, which is above 55. The oscar minimum of about 143 US gallons is also above 55.
