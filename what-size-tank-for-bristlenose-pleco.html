What Size Tank Does a Bristlenose Pleco Need?

Adult standard length 5 inches. A 31 × 12 inch base, about 19 US gallons, is enough for a couple; a group needs larger.

Quick answer: A bristlenose pleco (Ancistrus sp.) needs a minimum base of 31 × 12 inches, about 19 US gallons, which Seriously Fish calls sufficient for a couple of specimens, with a larger tank for a group, at an adult standard length of 5 inches. Filter at 290-360 liters per hour (76.62-95.11 gallons per hour) and keep 77-86°F with a 125-150 watt heater. About 19 gallons falls in the Aqueon 50 watt band (5-20 gallons) and the 100 watt band (15-40 gallons), both below the profile's 125-150 watts.

How to size it

Share the cory footprint, not the cory heater. Seriously Fish uses 31 × 12 inches and about 19 US gallons, and it says that is sufficient for a couple of specimens, with a larger tank for a group. The 20 gallon long search is the shopping match for a 31 inch base. Adult standard length is 5 inches, longer than the bronze cory's 3.0 inches, on the same floor.

290 ÷ 3.785 = 76.62 gph. 360 ÷ 3.785 = 95.11 gph.

Four times about 19 gallons is 76, and five times is 95. The converted rating meets that turnover. Nothing on the profile marks this flow as gentle.

The temperature is hot for a 19-gallon tank: 77-86°F, with 125-150 watts, assuming a room around 68°F. Aqueon still classifies about 19 gallons as a 50 watt tank (5-20) and a 100 watt tank (15-40). Both of those ratings are below 125. The 150 watt Aqueon band does not start until 30 gallons, so a 150 watt line on the species profile is not the same claim as Aqueon's 150 watt band. Report the gap; do not invent a wattage in between.
