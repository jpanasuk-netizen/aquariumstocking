Freshwater fish adult size and minimum tank footprint

Seriously Fish lists a neon tetra at 1.2 in. standard length with a 24 x 12 in. minimum tank base, a betta at 2.8 in. on an 18 x 12 in. base, an angelfish at 6 in. on a 39 x 16 in. base and an oscar at 14 in. on a 60 x 24 in. base.

Last updated: October 10, 2026.

Built by Jeremy Panasuk.

Lengths are standard length (SL), measured to the base of the tail, so they exclude the tail. Seriously Fish gives a minimum tank base footprint, not gallons, so this table does not convert to gallons. "Not recorded" means the species profile does not list that figure.
