How to size it

Seriously Fish prints the platy tank as a box, not only a floor: 24 × 12 × 12 inches, about 14 US gallons. The floor matches the neon tetra, cherry barb, and harlequin rasbora minimums. The 12 inch height is stated for the platy, as it is for the guppy and the molly. Adult standard length is 2.0 inches. No group size is printed in the figures used here, so none is added.

215 ÷ 3.785 = 56.80 gph. 270 ÷ 3.785 = 71.33 gph. That is the same liter rating as the neon tetra, the cherry barb, and the harlequin rasbora.

The temperature is the part that is not shared. Platies are listed at 68-79°F with a 50 watt heater, assuming a room around 68°F. Neon tetras are listed at 70-77°F, also on 50 watts. Cherry barbs run warmer on the heater line (50-75 watts) even though the volume is the same. Aqueon still sees one tank size: about 14 gallons is inside 5-20, so 50 watts is the listed band, and it is just short of 15, so the 100 watt band does not apply.

FAQ

What size tank do platies need?

Seriously Fish lists 24 × 12 × 12 inches, about 14 US gallons, for Xiphophorus maculatus. Adult standard length is 2.0 inches. The profile figures used here do not include a group count.

What filter rating is listed for platies?

Seriously Fish lists 215-270 liters per hour. Dividing by 3.785 gives 56.80-71.33 gallons per hour.

What heater wattage matches a platy tank of about 14 gallons?

Seriously Fish lists 68-79°F and a 50 watt heater, assuming a room around 68°F. Aqueon lists 50 watts for 5-20 gallons, which includes about 14 gallons. The 100 watt band starts at 15 gallons.
