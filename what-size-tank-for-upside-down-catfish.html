What Size Tank for Upside Down Catfish?

Check length and width against the tank on the species profile, then keep a group.

Full breakdown: freshwater fish size and tank data.

Quick answer: Seriously Fish lists the upside-down catfish (Synodontis nigriventris) at about 3.9 in. (100 mm) standard length and gives a 30 x 12 x 12 in. (70 litre) tank, at 75 to 82 °F and pH 6.0 to 7.5, noting it should be kept in a group. Aqueon's 20 gallon long measures 30¼ x 12½ in. across the base.

The Quick facts box lists aquarium size as not recorded, and the Aquarium size section lists aquarium base as not recorded. The tank in the quick answer is the next sentence in that section, printed as 30" x 12" x 12" (75x30x30cm) - 70 litres. The checker uses 30 in. by 12 in. from that sentence and does not convert the centimetre figures.

Check a tank against 30 × 12 in.

Group size

Seriously Fish says the upside-down catfish should be kept in groups of at least three or four, and in the behaviour section says groups of at least 3-4 individuals. In the aquarium size section, after the 30" x 12" x 12" tank, it says a single specimen could be kept in a smaller tank but this fish really should be kept in a group so a larger tank is required. The profile does not give a second set of dimensions for that larger tank.

Tank bases: Aqueon critical dimensions. A tank meets the length and width when it is at least 30 in. long and 12 in. front to back. Height is not part of this check. The 20 Long is 12¾ in. tall on that sheet; the profile's tank sentence is 12 in. tall.

What this means

Aqueon's 20 gallon long is long enough and wide enough. The 20 gallon high is the same 12½ in. front to back and only 24¼ in. long, so it misses the length. A 29 gallon has the same 30¼ × 12½ in. base as the 20 long. For a count of fish in that volume, see how many fish in a 20 gallon tank, and for the heater see what size heater for a 20 gallon tank.

Other small fish use a different footprint. See what size tank for corydoras and what size tank for a bristlenose pleco. The Quick facts row, with aquarium size left as not recorded, is on freshwater fish size and tank data.
