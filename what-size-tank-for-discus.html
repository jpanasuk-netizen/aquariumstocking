What Size Tank Does a Discus Need?

Adult standard length 6 inches, and they grow as tall as they are long. About 48 × 18 × 18 inches (255 liters) for a few juveniles or a breeding pair.

Full breakdown: how many fish per gallon.

Quick answer: Discus (Symphysodon aequifasciatus) need about 48 × 18 × 18 inches, published as 255 liters (255 ÷ 3.785 = 67.37 US gallons), for a few juveniles or a breeding pair, and they grow as tall as they are long at an adult standard length of 6 inches (Seriously Fish). No filter rating and no heater wattage are listed. Aqueon includes 67.37 gallons in both the 200 watt band (50-75 gallons) and the 300 watt band (65-90 gallons).

How to size it

Height is the sentence next to the tank, not a flourish. Seriously Fish gives about 48 × 18 × 18 inches (255 liters) for a few juveniles or a breeding pair, and says the fish grow as tall as they are long. Adult standard length is 6 inches, the same length printed for angelfish, on a different tank: the angelfish minimum is 39 × 16 × 20 inches and about 53 US gallons. Discus volume is published in liters.

255 ÷ 3.785 = 67.37 US gallons. Rounded to the nearest gallon, 67.37 is 67.

The shopping link searches a 67 gallon aquarium, that rounded gallon, not a claim that the profile printed 67. The 18 inch height matches the 18 inch width, which is the published way of saying the fish need a tall box. No headcount beyond "a few juveniles or a breeding pair" is printed, so none is added.

Filter liters and heater watts are not listed, so none are calculated from the four-to-five turnover rule. The heater figure that is available is the volume chart. Aqueon includes 67.37 in the 200 watt band because 67.37 is between 50 and 75, and in the 300 watt band because 67.37 is between 65 and 90. It is outside the 150 watt band, which ends at 55. A 55 gallon tank is short of 67.37; a 75 gallon tank contains it, because 67.37 is not above 75.

What filter and heater wattage does the discus profile list?

No filter rating and no heater wattage are listed for this profile. Aqueon's 200 watt band is 50-75 gallons, and the 300 watt band is 65-90 gallons. 67.37 gallons sits in both.

Does a 55 gallon tank meet the discus minimum?

No. The converted volume is 67.37 US gallons, which is above 55. It is inside Aqueon's 50-75 gallon band for 200 watts and inside the 65-90 gallon band for 300 watts.

Why does the shopping link say 67 gallons?
