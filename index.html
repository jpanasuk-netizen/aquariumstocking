Why bioload beats inches

The 1-inch-per-gallon rule dates from goldfish-bowl era advice. It ignores that a 10-inch oscar weighs 40× a 10-inch school of neons, that surface area limits oxygen exchange more than volume limits waste, and that adult size is what counts — the 2-inch bala shark you bought today wants 150 gallons in three years. Bioload points approximate real waste output: small (≤2") = 1 pt, medium (2–5") = 3 pts, large (5–10") = 8 pts, XL (10"+) = 14 pts, with messy fish (goldfish, common plecos) counted one class up.
