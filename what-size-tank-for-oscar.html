What Size Tank Does an Oscar Need?

Adult standard length 14 inches. A 60 × 24 inch base, about 143 US gallons, is just about large enough for a single adult.

Quick answer: An oscar (Astronotus ocellatus) needs a minimum base of 60 × 24 inches, about 143 US gallons, which Seriously Fish calls just about large enough for a single adult, with a larger tank for a pair or a group. Plan on two filters rated 1080-1350 liters per hour each (285.34-356.67 gallons per hour each) and 150-225 watts split across two heaters for 68-82°F. Aqueon stops at 300 watts for 65-90 gallons, so no Aqueon band contains about 143 gallons.

How to size it

Seriously Fish ties about 143 US gallons and a 60 × 24 inch base to one adult, at a standard length of 14 inches. The same sentence says a pair or a group needs a larger aquarium. This page stops at that single-adult minimum.

1080 ÷ 3.785 = 285.34 gph per filter. 1350 ÷ 3.785 = 356.67 gph per filter. The profile asks for two filters at that rating, not one.

Add the pair of filters to see total turnover. 285.34 + 285.34 = 570.68 gallons per hour at the low end, and 356.67 + 356.67 = 713.34 at the high end. Four times about 143 gallons is 572 gallons per hour, and five times is 715. Two filters in the published range land on the four-to-five rule for the whole tank. Sizing one filter to 285.34-356.67 gph and stopping there cuts that total in half relative to the printed equipment note.

Heat is also split. The profile lists 68-82°F and 150-225 watts across two heaters, assuming a room around 68°F. Aqueon publishes 50 watts for 5-20 gallons, 100 watts for 15-40, 150 watts for 30-55, 200 watts for 50-75, and 300 watts for 65-90. About 143 gallons is above 90, so it is outside every band on that chart. This page does not stretch the 300 watt line upward to cover 143 gallons.

FAQ

What size tank does an oscar need?

Seriously Fish lists a minimum base of 60 × 24 inches, about 143 US gallons, for Astronotus ocellatus, and calls that just about large enough for a single adult. A pair or a group needs larger. Adult standard length is 14 inches.

How much filtration does one adult oscar need?

Seriously Fish lists two filters at 1080-1350 liters per hour each. Dividing by 3.785 gives 285.34-356.67 gallons per hour each. Two filters at the low end add to 570.68 gallons per hour, and two at the high end add to 713.34. Four times about 143 gallons is 572, and five times is 715.

Does the Aqueon chart list a heater for about 143 gallons?

No. Aqueon's largest band is 300 watts for 65-90 gallons. About 143 gallons is above 90. Seriously Fish lists 68-82°F and 150-225 watts across two heaters, assuming a room around 68°F.
