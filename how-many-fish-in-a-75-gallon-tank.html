How Many Fish Can Go in a 75 Gallon Tank?

A 75 gallon tank is a 4 ft tank. Footprint decides which fish belong in it before you count heads.

Full breakdown: how many fish in a 10, 29 or 55 gallon tank.

Quick answer: Aqueon lists its standard 75 gallon tank at 48½ × 18½ in. across the base. Seriously Fish's minimum footprints for angelfish (39 × 16 in.), mollies and zebra danios (36 × 12 in.) and neon tetras (24 × 12 in.) all fit inside it. An oscar's 60 × 24 in. minimum does not. How to stock it

Footprint comes first. A fish needs swimming length and width, and depth does not make up for a short base. Each species above is compared on both length and width: angelfish need 39 × 16 in., which fits inside 48½ × 18½ in. An oscar needs 60 × 24 in., which is longer and wider than this tank, so it is the wrong tank even before counting. More on angelfish: what size tank for angelfish.

Count second. A footprint pass only says the species belongs in a tank this size. How many you can keep depends on adult size, the filter and how the species mix adds up, which is what the stocking calculator checks.
