What Size Tank Does a German Blue Ram Need?

Adult standard length 1.6 inches. About 14 US gallons on a 24 × 12 inch base is enough for a single pair, not a general community.

How to size it

The floor is the small schooling footprint, and the job of the tank is not a school. Seriously Fish says 24 × 12 inches, about 14 US gallons, is sufficient for a single pair, that the water should be pristine, and that the species is not recommended for a general community. Adult standard length is 1.6 inches. A neon tetra group of 8-10 uses the same base size for a different social plan; do not treat the ram minimum as a community quota.

110 ÷ 3.785 = 29.06 gph. 160 ÷ 3.785 = 42.27 gph. The profile marks this flow as gentle.

Four times about 14 gallons is 56 gallons per hour, and five times is 70. The gentle rating converts to 29.06-42.27 gph, well under that band, which is why a sponge filter fits these figures and a 56.80-71.33 gph filter (the neon tetra conversion of 215-270 liters per hour) does not. The betta gentle range is 21.14-33.03 gph, close to this one and still a different rating.

Heat is the other split. The profile lists 81-86°F and 125-175 watts, assuming a room around 68°F. Aqueon puts about 14 gallons only on the 50 watt line (5-20). The 100 watt line starts at 15, and 14 is below 15. A 125 watt species heater is above every Aqueon band that contains this volume. The high temperature is in the source; a corrected wattage is not, so none is calculated here.
