What Size Heater Does a 40 Gallon Tank Need?

40 gallons is listed under two Aqueon bands at once: 100 watts (15-40) and 150 watts (30-55).

Full breakdown: aquarium heater wattage guide.

Quick answer: A 40 gallon tank is covered by two Aqueon bands: the top of the 100 watt band (15-40 gallons) and inside the 150 watt band (30-55 gallons). The 200 watt band starts at 50 gallons, so it does not include 40. The species minimums that fit are the same ones that fit a 29, because the next minimum in this set is the angelfish at about 53 US gallons.

How to size it

This is an edge case the chart creates on purpose: neighboring bands share gallons. Aqueon lists 100 watts through 40 gallons and 150 watts from 30 gallons upward. A tank of exactly 40 gallons is on both lines.

40 is the last gallon of 15-40, and 30 ≤ 40 ≤ 55, so both 100 watts and 150 watts include a 40.

The next band does not join them. 200 watts is listed for 50-75 gallons, and 40 is below 50. 300 watts starts at 65. The 50 watt band ended at 20. So the overlap to explain is only 100 watts and 150 watts, and it exists because 40 is inside the shared region that runs from 30 gallons through 40 gallons. The chart does not say to add the watts together, and this page does not add them. It names both listings. The shopping links are a 100 watt heater and, as the next band up, a 150 watt heater.

No Seriously Fish minimum in this set lands between 29 and 40 gallons. The largest minimum that still fits is about 21 US gallons (zebra danio and molly). The next published volume is the angelfish at about 53, which is above 40, so moving from a 29 to a 40 does not unlock angelfish, discus, or oscar. Dwarf gourami volume is 56 liters. 56 ÷ 3.785 = 14.80 US gallons, still under 40, and that profile lists 72-81°F.

FAQ

What size heater does a 40 gallon tank need?

Aqueon lists 40 gallons under two bands: 100 watts for 15-40 gallons, and 150 watts for 30-55 gallons. The 200 watt band starts at 50 gallons and does not include 40.

Why do two wattages both include 40 gallons?

The 100 watt band ends at 40 gallons and the 150 watt band starts at 30 gallons. The shared stretch is 30 through 40 gallons, and 40 is the top of that stretch. Aqueon does not say to add 100 and 150 together.

Does a 40 gallon tank fit an angelfish?

No. Seriously Fish lists the angelfish minimum at about 53 US gallons, which is above 40. Discus converts to 67.37 US gallons and the oscar minimum is about 143 US gallons. Both are above 40.
