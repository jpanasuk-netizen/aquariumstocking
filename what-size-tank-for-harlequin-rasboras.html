What Size Tank Do Harlequin Rasboras Need?

Adult standard length 1.8 inches. Keep a group of at least 8-10 on a 24 × 12 inch base, about 14 US gallons.

Full breakdown: how many fish per gallon. Full breakdown: how many fish in a 20 gallon tank.

Quick answer: Harlequin rasboras (Trigonostigma heteromorpha) need a minimum base of 24 × 12 inches, about 14 US gallons, and a group of at least 8-10, at an adult standard length of 1.8 inches (Seriously Fish). The filter is 215-270 liters per hour (56.80-71.33 gallons per hour). Temperature is 70-82°F with a 75-100 watt heater, while Aqueon lists 50 watts for the 5-20 gallon band that contains about 14 gallons.

How to size it

Same floor as the cherry barb, different heater. Seriously Fish wants 24 × 12 inches, about 14 US gallons, and a group of at least 8-10. That headcount matches neon tetras, not the cherry barb's 6-10. Adult standard length is 1.8 inches, a little under the cherry barb's 2.0 inches and over the neon's 1.2 inches.

215 ÷ 3.785 = 56.80 gph. 270 ÷ 3.785 = 71.33 gph.

The filter box is the shared one: 215-270 liters per hour, ordinary four-to-five turnover on about 14 gallons (56 and 70 gallons per hour), not the ram's gentle 29.06-42.27 gph.

The profile then asks for more heat than the volume chart does. Listed temperature is 70-82°F with 75-100 watts, assuming a room around 68°F. Aqueon gives this volume the 50 watt band only, because 14 is inside 5-20 and below 15. 75 watts and 100 watts are on the species profile; they are not the Aqueon rows that contain 14 gallons. The 100 watt Aqueon row begins at 15 gallons. Both numbers stay on the page so the gap is visible.
