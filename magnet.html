1″ ≠ 1 gal

Inch-per-gallon is goldfish-bowl advice. Count waste, not inches.

A 10-inch oscar is not ten neon tetras. Adult size and mess class decide whether the tank stays cycled.

Points: small (≤2") = 1, medium (2–5") = 3, large (5–10") = 8, XL (10"+) = 14. On a normal setup, capacity equals the gallon count. A heavy-stock setup allows 0.6 points per gallon.

Use it in 10 minutes. Add the adult sizes you actually want, not the juveniles at the store. Stay under the tank's point cap. Heater watts below are this calculator for 68°F to 78°F. Filter ratings are the normal level: 5 turnovers, then a 20% step.
