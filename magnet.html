1″ ≠ 1 gal

Inch-per-gallon is goldfish-bowl advice. Count waste, not inches.

A 10-inch oscar is not ten neon tetras. Adult size and mess class decide whether the tank stays cycled.

Points: small (≤2") = 1, medium (2–5") = 3, large (5–10") = 8, XL (10"+) = 14. Messy fish (goldfish, common plecos) count one class up. Capacity ≈ gallons in points if filtration and water changes are real.

Use it in 10 minutes. Add the adult sizes you actually want, not the 1-inch juveniles at the store. Stay under the tank's point cap. Heater: roughly 3–5 W/gal for a 10°F rise. Filter: about 5× tank gallons per hour.
